Location

Montenegro: a mild climate between sea and mountains

The Adriatic coast has around 240 sunny days a year, frosts are rare in winter, and the cooler hills are less than an hour away in summer. Short distances and convenient walking routes make Montenegro a practical setting for a seasonal care programme.

Three climate zones

Three different climates within a short drive

Coast · 0–100 m

The Adriatic riviera

Mild winters, long spring and autumn seasons, sea air and pine trees. This is where the residence is located and where winter and spring stays are based.

Lakeland · 6–40 m

Lake Skadar

The largest lake in the Balkans is about an hour away. Calm water, birdlife and easy boat trips make it a good choice for a relaxed shoulder-season excursion.

Mountains · 900–1,700 m

Lovćen and the hinterland

In midsummer, the mountains are cooler than the coast. The drive takes about 40 minutes, and the bus goes directly to the viewpoints, so no long uphill walk is required.

Daily walks

Flat paths, benches, sea views

  • 6 km · level · benches throughout

    The Budva promenade

    From the marina to Bečići beach runs a level paved seafront promenade — ideal for the daily accompanied walking programme, with cafés and resting points every 200 metres.

  • 0.5 km · cobblestones · accompanied

    Budva old town

    Venetian lanes, small squares, and the citadel overlooking the sea. Compact enough for a morning; our carers know the step-free routes.

  • 1.5 km · park paths · shaded

    Miločer Park and Sveti Stefan

    Cypress and olive avenues of the former royal summer residence, plus the country's most famous photo view: the island fortress of Sveti Stefan — from the viewpoint, no descent required.

  • Year-round · at the residence

    Morning exercise by the water

    Part of the activity programme: supervised outdoor movement — in winter in the late morning, in summer early before the heat.

Excursions around the country

Montenegro without long walks or tiring transfers

Every excursion follows the same rules: an air-conditioned minibus gets as close as possible to the destination, walking sections stay under 300 metres, rest stops are planned, and a medically trained member of staff accompanies the group.

Half day · boat trip · UNESCO

Bay of Kotor

A fjord-like landscape seen from the water: Perast, the pilgrimage island of Our Lady of the Rocks, and Kotor's Venetian old town — the boat moors right at the promenade.

Half day · boat & tasting

Lake Skadar and the vineyards

A quiet boat ride between water-lily fields and monastery islands, followed by a tasting of Montenegrin wines and honeys at a family estate — all at ground level.

Half day · panoramic drive

Cetinje, the old royal capital

The historic capital with its monastery, royal palace and museums — plus the serpentine road overlooking the whole bay. A cultural programme without long walks.

Full day · the south · olive groves

Bar and Ulcinj

Europe's oldest olive tree (over 2,000 years), olive-oil tasting and the long sandy beaches of the south — an excursion for the warmer shoulder-season months.
