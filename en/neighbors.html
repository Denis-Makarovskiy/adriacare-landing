Across the border

Four neighbouring countries in our programme from Budva

Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Albania are within easy reach of Budva; North Macedonia works well for a short multi-day trip. We arrange door-to-door transport, short walking sections and medical support, and help the group through border formalities.

Day trips

Three countries: leave in the morning, return by evening

Croatia · 2 hrs · full day

Dubrovnik

See the city walls from below, walk along Stradun and take the cable car up Mount Srđ. The panoramic view requires no climb up steps.

Bosnia-Herzegovina · 90 min · full day

Trebinje

Plane-tree squares, Tvrdoš Monastery with a wine tasting and a small weekly market make a relaxed full-day programme.

Albania · 90 min · full day

Shkodra

See Rozafa Fortress from below, view Lake Skadar from the Albanian side and stop at a café on the pedestrian street.

Multi-day journeys

Multi-day trips with familiar care support

North Macedonia · 3 days · spring/autumn

Ohrid

The programme includes Ohrid’s UNESCO-listed old town, Sveti Naum Monastery and a boat trip to the springs. A care escort is available at the hotel around the clock.

Bosnia-Herzegovina · 2 days · shoulder season

Mostar & Herzegovina

The Old Bridge, the Blagaj dervish house at the source of the Buna, and an overnight stay in a quiet city hotel.
