Pilgrimage journeys

Montenegro’s holy sites, within easy reach of Budva

For many residents, faith remains an important part of life while they are away. We arrange accompanied visits to Orthodox and Catholic holy sites, allow time for rest and keep the pace unhurried. Attending a service is always optional.

Holy sites

The most important places of pilgrimage

90 min · full day

Ostrog Monastery

Ostrog is built into a sheer cliff above the Zeta valley and houses the relics of St Basil of Ostrog. The road reaches the upper monastery, so the visit does not require a long uphill walk.

40 min · half day

Cetinje Monastery

The monastery in Montenegro’s old royal capital houses the right hand of John the Baptist. The route from the minibus is short and level, and we allow plenty of time for the visit.

15 min · half day

Reževići Monastery

This small, quiet monastery stands among old olive trees near Petrovac. The main route is step-free, making it a comfortable place to pause for private prayer.

10 min · half day

Podmaine Monastery

Set above Budva with views across the bay, the monastery was once home to the bishop and poet Petar II Petrović-Njegoš. It is the closest monastery to the residence.

60 min · half day

Our Lady of the Rocks

The small island in the Bay of Kotor is home to a church dedicated to the Virgin Mary. The crossing from Perast takes only a few minutes, with seating on both the boat and the island.

50 min · half day

St Tryphon Cathedral, Kotor

The Catholic cathedral, founded in 1166, houses the relics of St Tryphon. It stands in the centre of Kotor old town, a short and mostly level walk from the pier.

Support

How we support each visit

We discuss the route with residents and their families in advance. We take account of each person’s ability and leave time for silence, a candle or prayer. Participation in services and rituals is always voluntary.

  • Coordination with the chaplain of your home before every journey
  • Attendance at the liturgy according to each person’s wish and strength
  • Planned seating and rest breaks on every route
  • Support in several languages; on request, we contact the local Orthodox, Catholic or Protestant community
