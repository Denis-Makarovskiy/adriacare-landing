Health & activity

Health and movement between sea and mountains

Regular outdoor movement is central to the programme: morning exercise on the terrace, balance work and walks on level mountain routes. Activities are adjusted to each resident’s ability, and a member of staff always accompanies the group.

At the residence

Daily activities

Daily · outdoors

Morning gymnastics & breathing exercises

Supervised sessions are adapted to mobility and care needs and include gentle strength work, stretching and breathing exercises. In winter they take place later in the morning; in summer, before the heat.

Several times a week · partner spa

Thalassotherapy & recovery

Our partner spa in Budva offers seawater pools, massage and physiotherapy. A doctor recommends the treatment; we arrange the minibus transfer and support.

Daily · garden & promenade

Memory and balance exercises outdoors

In small groups, residents work on balance, coordination and memory. The sessions support fall prevention, and progress is included in the report to the partner home.

Routes around Montenegro

Relaxed walking routes

Montenegro has level, well-maintained paths through mountain and lakeside landscapes. We assess every route for length, gradient, rest points and minibus access.

Responsibility

Safety during activity

Before each activity, a nurse checks how participants are feeling. Groups are limited to eight residents per escort. In summer we follow a hydration and shade plan, and a physiotherapist monitors individual exertion.
